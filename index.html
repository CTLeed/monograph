From the Editor

Monograph began as a reading list that refused to end. I kept a notebook of the products I found myself thinking about — the ones whose decisions stayed with me after I had stopped using them — and the notebook became a habit, and the habit became this.

Each Tuesday I publish one essay. The essay is about one piece of software. It is not a review. It is an attempt to understand a small number of choices, taken seriously, by people who were paying attention.

There are no sponsors in the essay itself. There is one tasteful line for a weekly underwriter at the top. Nothing else. Read slowly, forward generously, cancel gracelessly at any time.

— The EditorsBrooklyn, New York
