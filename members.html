Members Reading Room

Welcome, reader.

Every issue of Monograph, including the ones that have aged beyond the free six-month window. Quarterly long-form and Patron-only material will appear here as it publishes.

A note on this URL. This page is unlisted, not authenticated. Anyone with the link can read it. We share it only in the welcome email we send when a membership activates, and then once again every six months as a gentle reminder. If it begins circulating beyond the membership, we will rotate the URL and mail you the new one. Please do not share it publicly.

The complete archive

Every issue, oldest to newest, is available to you here. Issues published within the last six months are also available on the public archive page; older issues will, over time, live only here.

The quarterly long-form

Four times a year we publish a longer essay — usually 6,000 to 8,000 words on a single product or pattern. Each one will be emailed to members and archived here.

The first long-form will be published in summer 2026. This section will populate as the essays appear.

The annual book

Each December we print the year's issues as a hardcover. Patrons receive a copy with their name set in type on the inside cover; members can purchase additional copies at cost. We will email you in October to confirm shipping details.

Managing your membership

To pause, cancel, switch between monthly and annual, or update your card, the link in any Stripe receipt takes you to a customer portal. Or write to editor@monograph.press and we will sort it within the day.
